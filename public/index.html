Ludo Game Rules

🎲

Rolling a 6

Roll a 6 to bring a token out from the Yard to your Start Cell. Rolling a 6 also grants an Extra Roll! (3 consecutive 6s skips your turn).

⚔️

Capturing Opponents

Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their Yard and awards you an Extra Roll!

★

Safe Star Cells

The 8 cells marked with a star (★) are Safe Zones. Tokens on safe cells cannot be captured.

🏆

Home Stretch & Winning

After circling the board, move into your colored Home Corridor. Bring all 4 tokens to the Center to win!
